# Office of Community Safety -> Metro Public Health -- research notes

User testimony: staff was moved from the Office of Community Safety into
Metro Public Health Department, and subsequently quit or was fired. Not yet
independently documented by a news article found via search. What public
records DO show, as of 2026-09-05:

- Ron Johnson was named Nashville's first Community Safety Coordinator by
  Mayor Cooper in March 2021 (nashvillepost.com, newschannel5.com,
  meharryconference.com bio all agree on this).
- He does NOT appear anywhere in today's live Mayor's Office staff directory
  (captured this session as bytes/GOV-MAYOR-DIR.bin -- checked by string
  search, no hit for "Ron Johnson" or "Community Safety").
- His individual nashville.gov bio page,
  https://www.nashville.gov/departments/mayor/people/ron-johnson, returns
  HTTP 403 "Access denied" today -- the same Drupal pattern seen on
  GOV-MDHA-CA's now-gone page, consistent with an unpublished/removed staff
  record.
- A third-party directory (ZoomInfo) lists him as "Community Safety
  Coordinator at Metro Public Health Department" rather than the Mayor's
  Office -- weak evidence for an administrative move, since ZoomInfo scrapes
  can be stale or wrong, but notable that it names MPHD specifically.
- Nashville's current "Community Safety Plan" initiative is hosted at
  nashville.gov/departments/health/community-safety-plan and is described on
  that page as a partnership between the Mayor's Office and Metro Public
  Health Department, not as a health department program per se.
- A separate, newer "Crime Prevention and Violence Reduction Director" role
  was being recruited as of Sept 5, 2025 (Yahoo/local wire), tied to the
  mayor's campaign platform per Mayor's aide Brittany Irby -- this reads as a
  new/renamed role, not explicitly a direct Johnson replacement, and the
  article does not mention a predecessor leaving.

## What is NOT yet confirmed
