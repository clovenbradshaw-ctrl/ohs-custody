A junction is two statements from independent sources that share one exact value (a name, a figure, a full date or a street address) and each add something the other source never says. Sources that draw on one another or share an outlet are not independent. A pair is left out when any statement in the record already holds both sides, and values carried by many sources are left out as routine. Each junction is a lead to check against the documents, not a finding.
